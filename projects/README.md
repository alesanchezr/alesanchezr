Technical write-ups of systems, architectures, products and papers I have built. Each project has its own folder with a detailed document: the problem, the architecture, the decisions and trade-offs, and results with sources.

| 1 | [Caxton CMS](caxton/README.md) | 2025 to present | System, open source (MIT) | A Git-backed CMS where AI agents edit the website through the same validated pipeline as humans. Runs 4geeks.com. | Creator, lead engineer | Written |

## How each write-up is structured

1. **Summary**: what it is and the headline results.
2. **The problem**: why it had to exist.
3. **Architecture**: how it works, with diagrams.
4. **Key decisions**: what I chose, what I rejected, and why.
5. **Results**: numbers with their source and date.
6. **My role**: what I built and who I built it with.
7. **Evidence**: repos, live systems, docs.
